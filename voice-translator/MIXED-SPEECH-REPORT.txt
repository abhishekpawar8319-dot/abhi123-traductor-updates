WHAT WAS WRONG (version 1.6) AND WHAT CHANGED (1.7)
- Mixed sentences were given to one language by majority vote. "Pásame the wrench, please" counted as English, so Group
  mode saved it without translating (348 of these 1,000). Now: a sentence with any Spanish in it is translated into
  English; the English words pass through unchanged.
- English words inside Spanish sentences were marked orange as "maybe misheard" (821 of 1,000). Now only a single odd
  word is marked; real mixed speech (2+ words of the other language) gets no warnings.
- Mixed speech can make Google's recognizer report language errors; in Group mode each one made the app wait longer,
  up to 30 s without listening ("stuck"). Now: listen again after 0.15 s and drop the auto-detect options. A busy
  recognizer never waits more than 2 s.
- New: Auto-translate ON/OFF switch (OFF = record every sentence in full, translate only on request), → English and
  → Español buttons on every sentence, and "Translate all untranslated → English" in Saved translations.
